I'm new — show me how to connect
  1. Visit Aspire Builder Pro above, choose a plan and complete account setup. Confirm Social Planner and Private Integrations access with support.
  2. In your business workspace, open Marketing → Social Planner and connect the social channels you own.
  3. Open Settings → Private Integrations → Create new Integration. Name it ClipForge. Use your business/location, not an agency-wide token.
  4. Select the five permissions listed below. Copy the generated token into Private integration token.
  5. Find the location ID in your workspace URL: the text after /location/ and before the next /. Paste only that ID below.
  6. Click Test connection, select your staff name under Posting as, then Save settings.
  7. Render a clip, click Schedule, choose a connected channel, review the caption and choose a future time.

If a menu is missing, ask your Aspire administrator/support to enable it. Your social token and OpenRouter AI key are different.

Start here: Windows, Mac, branding and social setup ↗

  1. Click the ClipForge extension from any YouTube or video page.
  2. The private video engine starts automatically in the background.
  3. Choose local processing or enter your OpenRouter key in Settings.
  4. Generate, review your clips, adjust edits, and download MP4s or the ZIP.
  5. In Settings, save your GHL location ID and a Private Integration token with social planner + media scopes, then use Schedule or Calendar.

Authenticated or protected streams and blob URLs may not download. Upload your original video instead. Face tracking works best on clear, single-speaker footage; review crops before exporting.
